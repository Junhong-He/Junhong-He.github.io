I am currently a Master's student at [Beijing Institute of Technology](https://www.bit.edu.cn/), supervised by Prof. [Yan Yan](http://mc.bit.edu.cn/szdw/qzjs/yanyan/index.htm). I received my B.E. degree from [Shenzhen MSU-BIT University](https://www.smbu.edu.cn/) in 2024.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **AI image generation**, **3D reconstruction**, and **intelligent computing**. I have published 3 papers as first/co-first author in top-tier journals and conferences including **IEEE JSTAR (SCI Q1)**, **IEEE TCC (CCF-A)**, and **IEEE CAC (EI)**.
